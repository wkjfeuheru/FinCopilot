# FinHarness institution research desk

FinHarness is a Chinese financial-research copilot for individual researchers. The page's job is to move a user from a research question to a traceable, non-investment-advice conclusion.

Use a quiet institutional research-desk design: deep navy `#142638`, research teal `#176B63`, paper white `#F7F9FA`, blue-gray `#637487`, review amber `#A97620`, and status red `#B64444`. Use only system Chinese sans-serif typography; numbers and timings use a compact mono treatment. Avoid gradients, illustrations, fake charts, rounded-pillow cards, marketing hero motifs and speculative settings.

Preserve the desktop three-column structure: conversation history on the left, research conversation in the center, activity and sources on the right. The empty center state makes four research scenarios legible: equity, industry, macro, and quantitative-factor research. A single research-chain scale — question, plan, evidence, conclusion, review — is the signature visual feature. Complex-task plan steps use explicit red pending lamps, green complete lamps, red failed lamps and gray skipped markers, always paired with Chinese labels. Show only Chinese user-facing action names; never expose tool function names.
